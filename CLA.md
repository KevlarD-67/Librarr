# No CLA required

Librarr does **not** require a Contributor License Agreement.

Contributions are accepted under the project's outbound license, the
[GNU General Public License v3](LICENSE.md), under the standard
"inbound = outbound" principle: by submitting a patch (pull request,
issue attachment, etc.) you license that patch to the project and its
downstream users under the same GPL v3 terms the rest of the codebase
uses.

By contributing you affirm that:

1. You hold the copyright in your contribution, or are submitting it
   with the permission of the copyright holder.
2. You are entitled to license it under GPL v3.
3. Your employer (if any) has approved the contribution, or your
   contribution falls outside the scope of an assignment-of-rights
   employment clause.

That's it. There is no separate CLA form to sign, no rights
assignment, and no transfer of copyright to any individual or
organization.

## Heritage

The upstream Readarr project required a CLA assigning rights to the
Servarr team (see the project's git history for the previous text of
this file). Librarr has dropped that requirement to lower the
contribution barrier and to keep the codebase free of any
single-entity copyright concentration.

Contributions made to upstream Readarr under its CLA remain under
their original terms; this change only affects contributions made to
Librarr after the fork point.
